## Findings

Pass-with-findings — no blocking findings. The three re-review findings are resolved as claimed; the items below are follow-up observations that do not weaken the layout-data safety properties.

### 1. (verification record) Re-review finding 1 [P1] resolved; partition and display paths sanity-checked

- **Disjointness cannot be violated.** A candidate both placed and unplaced is rejected by the explicit intersection check (`OrganizationPlanMaterializer.kt` L131); union equality against the declared additions (L130) plus per-side duplicate checks (L127/L129) additionally forbid extra, missing, or duplicated ids, and a placement for an id outside snapshot ∪ additions is rejected in the partition loop itself (L120).
- **Unplaced candidates cannot reach the intended state or actions.** Only `candidatePlacements` are resolved into `candidateItems`; unplaced entries exist solely for validation and count projection. `partiallyPlacedCandidatesMaterializeOnlyThePlacedOnes` asserts the unplaced ref is absent from `plan.intendedState.items`, and the Insert list contains only the placed candidate. The apply-time `CandidateAvailabilityPort` re-verification consequently never sees an unplaced candidate.
- **The concrete-preview unplaced lines do not break the PreviewCounts rows/header truth contract.** `PreviewCounts` (projector) has no unplaced field and counts only materialized changes; the corpus header-equality contract (`PlanPreviewCountsCorpusContractTest`) and the content contract (`OrganizationPreviewContentTest`, 23 cases) are green. The unplaced lines come from the run `Summary.unplacedByReason` (informational context items, same rendering pattern as `warningCounts`) and are rendered in the preview, the rejection/Impossible surface, and the context surface alike — the placed Adds remain visible alongside the overflow, as the re-review requested.
- **NoChanges gate.** `allCandidatesUnplacedWithNoOtherChangesEndsInTheImpossibleSurface` pins the new routing; a zero-unplaced zero-change plan still ends in `State.NoChanges` byte-compatibly.

### 2. (verification record) Re-review finding 2 [P2] resolved; no exhaustive-when gaps

`PlanPreviewResult` has exactly seven variants (`Previewed`, `Stale`, `NotPlannable`, `CandidateResolutionFailed`, `Unavailable`, `WriterBusy`, `Concurrent`); the only production `when` over it (`ManualOrganizationRun.handlePlanPreview` L514-549) lists all seven without `else`, and the protocol's switch over the materializer's sealed `Result` is exhaustive over its three variants. Test fixtures (`NewFolderPlanFixtures`, `PreviewApplyPlacementEqualityTest`, `PreviewApplyPersistedPlacementEqualityTest`) already carried loud `CandidateResolutionFailed` branches from round 1. The enum relocation keeps the package direction (protocol → public imports only). `resolutionFailureOnTheFirstPreviewReachesTheTypedReDetectState` closes the prior record's Finding 4 (no run-level pin of the typed state): first-preview injection reaches `State.CandidateResolutionFailed` with the typed reason and zero apply calls.

### 3. (verification record) Re-review finding 3 [P2] resolved; journal invariant restored

`grep` confirms exactly two `USER_CANCELLED` emission sites (cancel, dismiss), both now gated on `Operation.journalStarted`, which is set immediately after the single `RUN_STARTED` emission in `runComposedPhase`.
